Privacy Policy

Lone Star Puzzle Game — Last updated: November 17, 2026

Overview

Lone Star ("the App") is a puzzle game developed by Alex Sehlke. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account, login, or registration, and we do not run our own servers. The App uses Apple services (Game Center and iCloud) for leaderboards and to sync your progress, and Google AdMob for advertising, as described below. We do not sell your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device to support gameplay:

  • Puzzle progress and completion status
  • Game settings and preferences (e.g., sound, haptics)
  • Statistics such as solve times, streaks, and hint usage

This data is stored in the device's local storage (UserDefaults).

iCloud Sync (iOS)

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App keeps your progress (completed puzzles and times, streaks, stickers, badges, statistics, and today's unfinished daily puzzles) the same on all your Apple devices by storing a copy in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot see or access it, and it is used only to sync your progress. Using “Reset All Progress” in the App's Settings erases your progress on all your devices and in iCloud. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your progress stays on your device only.

Leaderboards (Game Center and iCloud)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits your scores (such as daily puzzle solve times and puzzle counts) to Apple Game Center leaderboards. In addition, the App stores leaderboard entries in its public database in Apple iCloud (CloudKit) so that rankings can be shown in the App. These entries contain:

  • Your Game Center display name, player identifier, and profile picture
  • Puzzle completion counts, solve times, streaks, and win counts
  • The date of each entry

Leaderboard entries are visible to other players of the App. To show how many players completed each day's puzzles, the App also stores an anonymous record per completion with a random identifier that is generated by the App and not linked to you. To have your leaderboard entries removed, contact us at the address below.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • IP address (used for approximate location and fraud prevention)
  • General device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. We do not have access to personally identifiable information collected by AdMob.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking — disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Data from AdMob.

Network Access

All puzzles are bundled within the App and do not require an internet connection. The App connects to the internet to load advertisements via Google AdMob, and, when available, to Apple Game Center and iCloud for leaderboards and progress sync.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App uses Google AdMob which is configured to comply with applicable regulations. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
