Privacy Policy

Lone Star Puzzle Game (iOS) — Last updated: November 17, 2026

Overview

Lone Star ("the App") is a puzzle game developed by Alex Sehlke. This policy explains, in plain terms, what data the App collects, who receives it, why, and how you can control it. You don't need an account to play, we don't run our own servers, and we never sell your data.

Data leaves your device in only these ways:

  • Leaderboards — only if you are signed in to Game Center.
  • Advertising — Google AdMob shows ads in the free version.
  • iCloud sync — a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account, which we cannot see.
  • Anonymous statistics — anonymous counts used to show how many players solved today's puzzles and your overall rank.

1. Data That Stays on Your Device

Your puzzle progress, solve times, hints used, streaks, stickers, badges, statistics and settings (such as sound and haptics) are stored on your device. They are not sent to us.

2. iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App keeps your progress the same on all your Apple devices. It does this by storing a copy in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). The copy includes completed puzzles and times, streaks, stickers, badges, statistics and today's unfinished daily puzzles.

  • Who can see it: only you, through your Apple Account. Apple stores it; we cannot see or access it.
  • Why: only to sync your progress between your devices.
  • Your control: “Reset All Progress” in the App's Settings erases your progress on all your devices and in iCloud. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or turn iCloud off for the App, your progress stays on one device.

3. Leaderboards

This only applies if you are signed in to Game Center. If you are not, the App sends no leaderboard data.

What is collected

  • Your Game Center display name, player ID and profile picture
  • Your game results: daily puzzle solve times, puzzle counts, perfect and no-hint solves, streaks, Challenge and Battle Rush wins, constellations completed
  • The date of each result

Where it goes and why

  • Apple Game Center: your scores are submitted to the App's Game Center leaderboards. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.
  • The App's iCloud public database (Apple CloudKit): the App also stores your leaderboard entry there, so it can show daily and all-time rankings, your friends' rankings, and today's top solvers on the home screen. This database is run by Apple; as the developer, we can view it.
  • Fair daily rankings: your daily times are also used to check that each player gets only one ranked attempt per daily puzzle per day.

Who can see it

Other players of the App can see your name, profile picture and results in the leaderboards. Nobody else receives this data, and it is not used for advertising.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards exist. To stop new entries, sign out of Game Center (Settings > Game Center). To have your existing entries removed, see Delete Your Data or email us at the address below.

4. Anonymous Statistics

The App stores two kinds of anonymous records in its iCloud public database. Both use a random number that the App creates on your device instead of any name, Game Center ID or Apple Account, so they are not linked to you:

  • Daily solves: one record per daily puzzle you solve, holding the puzzle tier and the date. Used to show how many players solved each day's puzzles.
  • Total puzzles solved: one number, updated as you play. Used to show your overall rank among all players.

These work without Game Center but need iCloud; without iCloud nothing is sent.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads from Google AdMob. The AdMob software in the App sends data directly to Google when it loads and shows an ad. We do not receive this data, and we never pass your leaderboard data, game results or Game Center details to Google.

What AdMob collects, and why

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you allow tracking (see below). Used to show and measure ads.
  • Approximate location: worked out from your IP address. Used to show ads relevant to your region and to prevent fraud. AdMob does not use GPS.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown or tapped, and app launches. Used to show ads and measure how they perform.
  • Crash, performance and diagnostic data: from the ad software itself. Used to fix problems and measure ad performance.

Tracking and your choice

The first time you open the App, iOS asks whether the App may track you. “Tracking” means Google may combine data from this App with data from other companies' apps and websites to personalize ads.

  • Allow: ads may be personalized to your interests.
  • Ask App Not to Track: AdMob cannot read your advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. You see the same number of ads either way.

You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Buying Pro in the App turns ads off completely.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Data from AdMob.

6. What We Don't Collect

  • No email address, phone number or contacts
  • No precise (GPS) location
  • No photos from your library; the only picture used is your Game Center profile picture, for the leaderboards
  • No payment details: purchases are handled entirely by Apple
  • No sale of data, and no data brokers

7. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The App only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock it. We never see your payment details.

8. Network Access

All puzzles are built into the App and play offline. The App goes online only to load ads, to use Game Center and the leaderboards, and to sync through iCloud.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be shown on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
